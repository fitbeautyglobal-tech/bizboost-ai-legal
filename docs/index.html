1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the BizBoost AI mobile application ("Application", "App", "we", "us", or "our"), published by [INSERT DEVELOPER / COMPANY NAME] (contact: [INSERT SUPPORT EMAIL]).

BizBoost AI is an AI-powered marketing assistant designed to help small businesses, freelancers, online merchants, and creators draft promotional copy, social captions, product descriptions, and marketing calendars. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the application's core functionality:

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and password. Passwords are cryptographically hashed and managed securely through our backend authentication provider (Supabase Auth). We never see or store plain-text passwords.
  • User Profile Data: Display name and business role (e.g., Freelancer, Agency Owner, Online Seller) stored in your user profile to personalize your experience.
  • Brand Profiles: Brand name, industry, target customer demographics, brand voice/tone, products/services descriptions, and website URL that you voluntarily provide to customize generated marketing content.
  • Saved Content & Generation History: Marketing copy, captions, advertising copy, product descriptions, and content calendar records that you generate and choose to save to your cloud library or device.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Standard technical information during secure HTTPS calls (such as IP address, operating system version, and timestamps) solely for network transmission, server health, and quota management.
We Do Not Collect: BizBoost AI does NOT request or access GPS location, device contacts, camera hardware, microphones, photo libraries, device storage (no broad storage permissions), or biometric identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  1. Authenticating Your Account: To log you in, maintain your active session, and ensure your saved content is accessible only to you.
  2. Generating Marketing Copy: To pass your prompt topics, brand context, and chosen parameters to our secure server-side AI processing function to produce relevant marketing variations.
  3. Synchronizing Your Content: To store your saved items and brands securely in your personal cloud library so you do not lose your marketing work across app sessions.
  4. Enforcing Usage Limits: To track monthly AI generation credits to prevent abuse and protect free service infrastructure.

4. How AI Processing Works (Google Gemini Integration)

BizBoost AI utilizes Google Gemini 2.5 Flash through a secure server-side Supabase Edge Function to process marketing generation requests.

  • Transmission: Only the marketing topic, brand context (if provided), tone, and platform parameters are sent securely over HTTPS to the Edge Function.
  • No Secret Exposure: Server API keys and authorization credentials reside strictly on the server and are never included in the Android application package or device memory.
  • AI Output Notice: Generated marketing content is produced by artificial intelligence and may contain errors. You are encouraged to review all AI-generated copy before publishing it to your public marketing channels.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your records:

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Our database enforces zero-trust Row Level Security rules (auth.uid() = user_id). Each user can read, create, update, or delete only their own private records.
  • HTTPS / TLS Only: All client-to-server communications are strictly encrypted in transit using HTTPS (cleartext traffic is disabled in Android).
  • Backup Restrictions: Device backup extraction is disabled (android:allowBackup="false") to protect stored session tokens.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers.

Your data is only processed by essential technical infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: For cloud authentication, database persistence, and serverless Edge Function hosting.
  • Google AI (Gemini): For real-time inference on prompts submitted through the marketing generator.

  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion by emailing [INSERT SUPPORT EMAIL] or visiting our Account Deletion Instructions page.
