Privacy Policy

Last Updated / Effective Date: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the BizBoost AI mobile application ("Application", "App", "we", "us", or "our"), published by BizBoost AI (contact: amiraistudiox@gmail.com).

BizBoost AI is an AI-powered marketing assistant designed to help small businesses, freelancers, online merchants, and creators draft promotional copy, social captions, product descriptions, and marketing calendars. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the application's core functionality:

  • Demo Mode (Local-Only): If you use BizBoost AI in Demo Mode without creating an account, operations, generated variations, and test data are intended to remain on your local device. Account-based cloud features may require signing in.
  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and password. Passwords are managed through our backend authentication provider (Supabase Auth). We do not store plain-text passwords.
  • User Profile Data: Display name and business role (e.g., Freelancer, Agency Owner, Online Seller) stored in your user profile to personalize your experience.
  • Brand Profiles: Brand name, industry, target customer demographics, brand voice/tone, products/services descriptions, and website URL that you voluntarily provide to customize generated marketing content.
  • Saved Content & Generation History: Marketing copy, captions, advertising copy, product descriptions, and content calendar records that you generate and choose to save to your cloud library or device.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Standard technical information during secure HTTPS calls, such as IP address, operating system version, and timestamps, may be processed for network transmission, server health, and service management.
Advertising and Tracking: BizBoost AI is designed to provide its core marketing features without advertising. Technical information may still be processed by essential service providers to operate and secure the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  1. Authenticating Your Account: To log you in, maintain your active session, and provide access to your account features.
  2. Generating Marketing Copy: To process your prompt topics, brand context, and chosen parameters through our server-side AI processing function to produce marketing variations.
  3. Synchronizing Your Content: To store saved items and brand information in your cloud library where this feature is available.
  4. Enforcing Usage Limits: To track AI generation usage and help prevent abuse and protect service infrastructure.

4. How AI Processing Works (Google Gemini Integration)

BizBoost AI uses Google Gemini 2.5 Flash through a server-side Supabase Edge Function to process marketing generation requests.

  • Transmission: Marketing topics, brand context (if provided), tone, and platform parameters may be sent securely over HTTPS to the Edge Function for processing.
  • API Key Protection: Server-side API keys are intended to remain on the server and are not intended to be exposed in the Android application package.
  • AI Output Notice: Generated marketing content is produced by artificial intelligence and may contain errors. You should review all AI-generated copy before publishing it to public marketing channels.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect your information.

  • Database Access Controls: Access to cloud records is managed through authentication and database security policies intended to restrict users to their own records.
  • HTTPS / TLS: Client-to-server communications use HTTPS encryption in transit.
  • Device Security: We use application security settings intended to help protect locally stored information and session data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties or data brokers.

Your information may be processed by essential technical service providers, including:

  • Supabase: For cloud authentication, database services, and serverless Edge Function hosting.
  • Google AI (Gemini): For AI processing of marketing generation requests submitted through the application.

7. In-App User Rights & Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your BizBoost AI account using the following methods:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can request account deletion directly inside the app by going to Settings & Account > Delete My Account. After you confirm, the app processes the deletion request and signs you out automatically. Associated account information and user data are handled according to our deletion process and applicable legal and technical requirements. Some limited information may remain in backups or be retained where required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion by emailing amiraistudiox@gmail.com or visiting our Account Deletion Instructions page.

8. Children's Privacy

BizBoost AI is a commercial productivity and marketing tool intended for business professionals, adult freelancers, and online sellers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.
