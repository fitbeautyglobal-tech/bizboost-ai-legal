🚀 Native Android Marketing Assistant

BizBoost AI – AI Marketing Tools for Small Businesses

A mobile marketing assistant designed to help small business owners, freelancers, online sellers, and agencies draft social media posts, brainstorm ad copy, write product descriptions, and organize growth strategies. Users should review AI-generated content before publishing.

Publication Status: BizBoost AI is currently in active pre-release development for Android. The application is not yet publicly available on Google Play. Availability updates will be published on this portal.

Core Features & Capabilities

BizBoost AI provides tools to assist with content drafting, product descriptions, and organic search planning in one mobile application.

✍️

AI Writer

Generate audience-specific social posts, engaging hooks, reel outlines, and carousel scripts tailored to your specific brand tone and promotional goals.

📱

Social Media Posts

Format marketing copy formatted for major platforms including Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X, complete with relevant hashtag suggestions.

📢

Ad Creator

Craft punchy headlines, clear value propositions, primary ad copy, and action-oriented CTAs designed for Meta Ads and digital marketing campaigns.

🛍️

Product Descriptions

Create search-friendly titles, bulleted feature highlights, and persuasive product descriptions for online stores, Shopify catalogs, and marketplace listings.

📅

30-Day Content Planner

Organize your month with a daily content roadmap organized around balanced marketing pillars: educational tips, promotional offers, social proof, and engagement.

📈

Growth & ASO Studio

Conduct qualitative keyword research, validate Play Store metadata against strict character rules (titles ≤ 30 chars, short descriptions ≤ 80 chars), and organize website SEO structures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding BizBoost AI's features, platform status, and operational guidelines.

What is BizBoost AI?

BizBoost AI is an Android application designed to assist businesses and creators with marketing tasks, including social media post drafting, paid ad copywriting, product SEO descriptions, calendar planning, and store discoverability analysis.

Is BizBoost AI available for download on Google Play?

Not yet. BizBoost AI is currently in active pre-release development and is not yet publicly available on Google Play. There is no public download link available at this time. Availability updates will be provided on this verified website.

Does BizBoost AI guarantee marketing, ranking, or sales results?

No. BizBoost AI helps users draft marketing ideas, organize keyword suggestions, and structure content plans. Search engines and social platform algorithms determine visibility independently, and no marketing tool can guarantee rankings, traffic, or sales. Users should always review and refine AI-generated drafts prior to publishing.

How does BizBoost AI handle user data and privacy?

For detailed information about how user accounts, authentication, and submitted prompts are processed and stored, please consult our official Privacy Policy.

How can I request deletion of my account or data?

You can delete your account and associated data directly within the mobile application settings, or submit a request via our official Account Deletion Instructions page or by contacting our support team by email.
