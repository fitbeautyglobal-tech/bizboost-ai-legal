How to Delete Your BizBoost AI Account & Data

Instructions for deleting your account and requesting removal of associated information.

Important: Account deletion is a significant action. Once you confirm deletion in the application, you will be signed out of your account. Please make sure you understand the consequences before proceeding.

Method 1: Delete Your Account in the App

You can delete your BizBoost AI account from within the Android application by following these steps:

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Open the BizBoost AI application on your Android device.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open the Settings & Account section.
  4. Find and tap the Delete My Account option.
  5. Read the deletion warning carefully.
  6. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

After you confirm the deletion, the application processes the account deletion request and signs you out automatically. You will need to create or use another account if you want to use account-based features again.

Method 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you have uninstalled the application, lost access to your device, or cannot use the in-app deletion option, you can contact our support team to request account deletion.

Email Request Instructions:

  1. Send an email to: amiraistudiox@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: BizBoost AI - Account Deletion Request
  3. Send the request from the email address associated with your BizBoost AI account, where possible.
  4. Clearly state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are the account owner before processing the request. Our support team will respond about the next steps.

Information Covered by a Deletion Request

Depending on the information associated with your account and the applicable technical and legal requirements, a deletion request may cover:

  • Account information, such as your registered email address and user profile.
  • Profile details, such as display name and business role.
  • Saved brand profiles and related business information.
  • Saved marketing content and custom templates.
  • AI-generated content history associated with your account.

Data Retention and Deletion

We process account deletion requests to remove the associated account information and user data from our active systems, subject to applicable technical and legal requirements.

Certain limited information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or to resolve disputes. Data in backup systems may remain for a limited period until those backups are routinely overwritten or deleted.

For more information about how BizBoost AI handles personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.
