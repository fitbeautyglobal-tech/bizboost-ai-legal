How to Delete Your BizBoost AI Account & Data

Clear, transparent instructions on permanent account deletion and data removal.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All saved marketing items, custom brand kits, generated content history, and profile settings will be immediately and irreversibly purged from our servers.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can delete your account directly from within the BizBoost AI mobile application at any time:

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Open the BizBoost AI app on your Android device.
  2. Make sure you are signed in to the account you wish to delete.
  3. Navigate to the Settings screen (tap the gear icon on the dashboard).
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen.
  5. Tap the "Delete My Account" button (outlined in red).
  6. Read the warning prompt and confirm by tapping "Permanently Delete".

Once confirmed, the app immediately communicates with our backend to delete your authentication record and all linked database rows, signs you out, and clears all local session data.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app, lost your device, or cannot access your account, you can request manual deletion via email:

Email Request Instructions:

  1. Send an email to: amiraistudiox@gmail.com
  2. Use the Subject line: "BizBoost AI - Account Deletion Request"
  3. Send the email from the same email address associated with your BizBoost AI account.
  4. State clearly in the message that you request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

We will verify your request and process the complete deletion within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been purged.

What Data Is Deleted Upon Request?

When your account is deleted, the following records are permanently destroyed:

  • Your authentication credentials (email address, password hash, and user ID).
  • Your profile information (display name, business role).
  • All saved Brand Profiles (company descriptions, target audience, brand voices).
  • All saved marketing content and custom templates in your library.
  • All AI generation history logs linked to your user account.

Data Retention Period

In-app deletion takes effect immediately on production databases. For email requests, processing takes up to 7 business days. We do not retain backup copies of deleted user content once the server purge has completed.
